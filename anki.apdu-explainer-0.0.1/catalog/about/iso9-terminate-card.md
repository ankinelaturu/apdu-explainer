TERMINATE CARD USAGE is the last life-cycle command: the card (or MF) is permanently taken out of service. There is no ISO reactivate.

Issuer-defined remnants may still answer ATR but reject application commands (`6A81` / `6D00`).

## Status words you will see

| SW | Meaning |
|----|---------|
| `90 00` | Terminated |
| `6982` | Not allowed |
